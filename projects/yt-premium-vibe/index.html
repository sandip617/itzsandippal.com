Mac · about 5 minutes

Install on Safari

No Xcode needed. You download a small Mac app, allow it once, and switch the extension on in Safari.

  1. Download the appClick “Download for Mac” at the top of this page. Open your Downloads folder and double-click YT-Premium-Vibe-for-Mac.zip to unzip it.
  2. Move it to ApplicationsInside the unzipped folder, drag YT Premium Vibe.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it onceDouble-click the app in Applications. macOS will say it can’t verify the app, because it isn’t sold through the App Store. Click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to “YT Premium Vibe was blocked”. Enter your Mac password and click Open. A small window appears. You can close it.
    Prefer Terminal? Paste this, press Enter, then open the app:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/YT Premium Vibe.app"
  4. Show Safari’s developer settingsSafari → Settings → Advanced → tick Show features for web developers.Show me how →
  5. Allow the extensionSafari → Settings → Developer → tick Allow unsigned extensions and enter your password. Show me how →
    Safari switches this off every time you quit Safari (⌘Q) or restart your Mac. After a restart, tick it again. Your settings stay saved. Want it to stay on for good? Sign it once (free) →
  6. Turn it onSafari → Settings → Extensions → tick YT Premium Vibe.Show me how →
  7. Allow it on YouTubeOpen youtube.com, click the new YT Premium Vibe icon next to the address bar, and choose Always Allow on This Website. Reload the page. You’ll now see AI Summary and Download above the Like button.Show me how →

Free · 2 minutes

Set up AI Summary

AI Summary uses Google Gemini’s free tier, so you need your own free key. It’s stored only on your Mac.

  1. Get a keyGo to aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with your Google account, click Create API key and copy it.
  2. Paste itOn any YouTube video, click AI Summary. A panel opens on the right. Paste the key and click Save & summarize.
  3. Study with itYou get the video explained chapter by chapter, with key terms and exam-style questions. Click বাংলা at the top for Bangla. Click any timestamp to jump to that moment, and ask follow-up questions in the chat box.

One-time setup

Download videos

Click Download above the Like button and choose Best quality video or Audio only. Files go to your Downloads folder.

  1. First downloadThe first time, the extension sets up its downloader by itself. This takes a few seconds. You’ll see the progress on the button, then “Done ✓” with a Show in Finder button.
  2. If you see “One-time setup”Some Macs need one extra tool. Click Copy setup command in that message, open Terminal (⌘ + Space, type Terminal), paste, and press Enter. It may ask for your password and take a few minutes. Then click Download again. This is the same command:
    command -v brew >/dev/null || /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"; eval "$(/opt/homebrew/bin/brew shellenv 2>/dev/null || /usr/local/bin/brew shellenv)"; brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg
  3. Change the folderClick the toolbar icon → Download → ⌄ and type another folder, for example ~/Movies/YouTube.

Everything inside

What it does

Click the toolbar icon to turn each feature on or off. Changes apply instantly.

Ad Blocker

Skips video ads, hides banner and feed ads, and removes “ad blocker” warning popups.

Sponsor Skip

Jumps over sponsor segments using SponsorBlock, with an Undo button.

Hide Shorts

Removes Shorts from home, search, the sidebar and subscriptions.

AI Summary

Chapter-by-chapter study notes in English or Bangla, plus a chat for follow-up questions.

Download

Best-quality video or audio only, saved straight to your Mac.

Custom Speed

S = slower, D = faster, R = normal. From 0.25× to 4×, remembered per channel.

Smart Quality

Highest quality in fullscreen, 720p in normal view, switching automatically.

Volume Boost

Up to 300% louder with a slider in the player, without distortion.

Picture-in-Picture

One click floats the video over your other windows.

Cinema Mode

Softly blurs everything around the video. Hover an area to clear it.

Auto Audio Track

Picks Bangla, then Hindi dubbed audio when a video has it. You can reorder this.

Stuck?

If something goes wrong

YT Premium Vibe isn’t in Safari’s Extensions list

Open the app from Applications once, then check that Safari → Settings → Developer → Allow unsigned extensions is ticked.

It disappeared after restarting Safari or my Mac

Tick Allow unsigned extensions again (Safari → Settings → Developer). This is a Safari rule for extensions that aren’t signed. To stop it for good, sign the app once with your free Apple ID.

Show me how →
No buttons on YouTube

Safari → Settings → Websites → Extensions → YT Premium Vibe → set youtube.com to Allow, then reload YouTube.

A video won’t play

If you also use another ad blocker (like uBlock), turn one of them off. Or open the toolbar icon → Ad Blocker → turn off “Remove ads from player data”.
