# Sandip Pal Portfolio

## Upload to Hostinger

1. Extract this folder.
2. Open Hostinger hPanel → File Manager.
3. Open your domain's `public_html` folder.
4. Upload:
   - `index.html`
   - `style.css`
   - `script.js`
   - the entire `assets` folder
5. Make sure `index.html` is directly inside `public_html`.
6. Visit your domain.

## Personalize before publishing

Open `script.js` and replace:
- `your@email.com`
- `919999999999`

Add your photo:
- Put your photo at `assets/profile.jpg`

Add your CV:
- Put your PDF at `assets/Sandip-Pal-CV.pdf`

You can replace the four sample portfolio cards in `index.html` with your real projects.

## Notes

This version is static and works on normal Hostinger shared hosting without a backend.
The contact form uses the visitor's email app via `mailto:`. For a true website form that stores/sends submissions without opening an email app, connect Formspree, Web3Forms, Hostinger Email/SMTP, or a custom backend later.
